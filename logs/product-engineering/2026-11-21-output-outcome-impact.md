# Product Engineering: Output vs outcome vs impact
- **Date:** 2026-11-21 (unscheduled, ledger U-001). Minutes not recorded.
- **Roadmap:** PE Week 1, W01.1
- **Status:** done (explain-back passed)

## Explain-back (summary of his answer)
- Output = the shipped deliverable. Outcome = measurable change in user behavior caused by it; a feature nobody uses is an output with a failed outcome. Impact = business value of the outcome (retention, clients, revenue).
- Bloopo example: one-tap "message seller" from the item page (output) → buyers message sellers from the item page, measured by the % of buyers messaging, before vs after (outcome) → retention / user base growth (impact).
- Why it matters: a product engineer focuses on outcomes; lots of output can create no value.

## Feedback
- Deeper outcome: item-page view → conversation → reservation/purchase. Message rate is a leading indicator.
- Before/after comparisons are confounded; at Bloopo's traffic, combine them with buyer conversations. A/B testing is covered in Week 14.
- Impact: add marketplace liquidity (successful connections keep sellers, which brings more listings and more buyers).
